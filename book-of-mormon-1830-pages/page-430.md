## CHAPTER III.

### THE PROPHECY OF NEPHI,

THE SON OF HELAMAN.

*God threatens the people of Nephi, that he will visit them in his anger, to their utter destruction, except they repent of their wickedness. God smiteth the people of Nephi with pestilence; they repent and turn unto him. Samnel, a Lamanite, prophesies unto the Nephites.*
