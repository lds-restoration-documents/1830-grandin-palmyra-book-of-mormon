# Transcription notes — 1830 Grandin Palmyra Book of Mormon

Companion to the `book-of-mormon-1830-pages/` folder (one markdown file per PDF page). This file records how the text was produced, the conventions used, and every place where the transcription is doubtful, illegible, or reflects a judgment call. Structure follows `../1828-phinney-cooperstown-bible/transcription-notes.md`. **Extraction is complete: all 598 PDF pages are transcribed, audited once against the scan, and then proofread a second time, blind, against fresh 4x renders (§6b). The 76 close calls were reviewed by a person (§6c); the book as a whole has not been proofread by a person.**

## 1. Source and method

- **Source:** `Book-of-Mormon-1830.pdf` (repo root, gitignored) from https://archive.org/details/book-of-mormon-1830 (University of Pittsburgh, Darlington Memorial Library copy). The scan is 598 PDF pages (about 367 x 659 pt each, single column; an Internet Archive digitization with a Pitt library conservation sheet near the end).
- **Layout (PDF pages):** 1 marbled endpaper with a cloth binding strip; 2 Pittsburgh Darlington Memorial Library bookplate (arms, motto CRUCE DUM SPIRO SPERO, typed shelfmark Dar. BX8623 1830); 3 blank endpaper; 4 Internet Archive digitization notice (identifier `bookofmormonacco1830smit`); 5 title page; 6 title-page verso (copyright notice, District of New-York, 11 June 1829; the scan is cropped at the right and slightly at the left edge, so line ends are cut off); 7–8 Preface (7 has no printed folio and the signature mark "1*"; 8 is folio IV; "THE AUTHOR." closes it, lower half blank); **9–592 the Book of Mormon text** (1 Nephi opens on 9, printed p. 5; printed page = PDF − 4; the Second Book of Nephi opens mid-page on 63 (printed 59), the Book of Jacob at the top of 127, Enos mid-page on 147, Jarom at the top of 150, Omni at the top of 152 the Words of Mormon mid-page on 155 (continuing to the foot of p. 157) the Book of Mosiah at the foot of 157 the Book of Alma mid-page on 225 and the Book of Helaman mid-page on 411 and the Book of Nephi (the third book of that name) at the top of 456, a further Book of Nephi mid-page on 518 and the Book of Mormon mid-page on 522, the Book of Ether mid-page on 542 and the Book of Moroni at the top of 578; Moroni ends on 592 with "THE END,"); 593 Testimony of Three Witnesses; 594 Testimony of Eight Witnesses; 595–596 blank; 597 library conservation record; 598 back cover. (Boundaries after 8 inferred from the embedded OCR; every page verified against the images.)
- **Embedded OCR** exists for most pages but is very noisy (e.g. "Sariali", "mnsT BOOK of nephi"); it is a helper only.
- **Not in this edition:** verse numbers, marginal notes, reference letters. It has italic chapter summaries, long paragraphs, and a thin space before `; : ? !` (not transcribed: the files have no space there; decided after the first audit showed it could not be judged reliably).
- **Process:** each page is rendered at 2x and cut into two overlapping crops (top/bottom) plus a low-resolution overview. An AI model reads the crops and the embedded OCR text (if any) and writes the page. The image is authoritative; the OCR text is only a helper. Pages are processed in batches of 16.
- **Models:** All 598 PDF pages were written by Claude Sonnet 5.5: pages 9–11 directly in the main session (pilot), the rest by subagents in batches of 16 (12–27, 28–43, 44–59; then 60–75, 76–91, 92–107 and the front matter 1–8 in parallel; then 108–123, 124–139, 140–155 in parallel; then 156–171, 172–187, 188–203 in parallel; then 204–219, 220–235, 236–251 in parallel; then 252–267, 268–283, 284–299 in parallel; then 300–315, 316–331, 332–347 in parallel; then 348–363, 364–379, 380–395 in parallel, their first audit run being cut off by a weekly rate limit and rerun in full after the reset; then 396–411, 412–427, 428–443 in parallel; then 444–459, 460–475, 476–491 in parallel; then 492–507, 508–523, 524–539 in parallel; then 540–555, 556–571, 572–587 and 588–598 in parallel, the 540–555 agent being stopped by an API content-filter error after page 542 and its remaining pages 543–555 rerun by a second agent).
- **Review:** (1) a scripted structural check (page comment matches filename, `<!-- cont -->` joins, no space before `; : ? !`, compile runs); (2) pages 1–598 were each **audited page by page against both crops of the scan** by a second Sonnet 5.5 subagent (see §6), with zoomed 5x renders for doubtful spots; (3) the main session spot-read pages 13 and 25 against the images. No page has been proofread by a person.

## 2. Conventions

See `tools/transcription-spec.md`. In short: one file per PDF page starting `<!-- PDF page NNN -->`; `**Header:**` running head; `#` book titles, `## CHAPTER X.`; one paragraph per printed paragraph with `<!-- cont -->` marking a paragraph continued from the previous page; `*italic*` as printed; line-end hyphenations joined; spelling, punctuation and printer's errors kept as printed; no space before `; : ? !`; doubtful readings `<!-- uncertain: … -->`, unreadable `[illegible]`; signature marks / catchwords on a `Footer:` line.

## 6a. Known limits

- Every page was transcribed by one Sonnet 5.5 agent and audited once by another; where the two disagreed the audit's reading (checked against zoomed images) was taken. A third, blind reader then covered every page (§6b).
- The borderline calls listed in §6 are visual judgments: commas versus periods versus specks on worn type, blots over letters, a thin space or hyphen at a line end. They affect punctuation, not words, with a few exceptions (p. 158 "king..", p. 387 "Nephihah..", p. 486 "your").
- The thin space the type puts before `; : ? !` is not transcribed (§3). Printed folios and running heads are kept as printed, errors included.
- The embedded OCR in the PDF was only a helper and never the source of a reading.

## 6d. Printer's-typo catalog

`printer-typos.md` lists the likely printer's typos and misspellings (about 140 occurrences: letters dropped, added, transposed or substituted, repeated syllables, run-together or doubled words, recurring misspellings, and a few name variants), each with its PDF page, printed page and context, and ends with the categories of spelling that were deliberately left out as normal or intentional (`shew`, `-eth` forms, King James-era spellings like `marvellous`, grammar of the dictated text, the Alma 11 measures). It was built from a dictionary scan of the whole compiled text (words not in `/usr/share/dict`, with an inflection-aware check), triaged by hand, and is generated by `tools/make_typo_catalog.py`. It is likely, not exhaustive: a slip that yields another real word is only caught where it was noticed. All 142 occurrences were then checked against the scan at standard size by four separate passes (110 pages): none differed from the print; three were not settled at that size and rest on the earlier zoomed audits (`utttered` p. 499, `wben` p. 577, `Mulok` p. 211).

## 7. Compiled text

`book-of-mormon-1830.md` (about 273,400 words) is generated by `tools/compile_markdown.py`: it removes comments, running heads, footers and marginal-note sections, and rejoins paragraphs marked `<!-- cont -->`; a `<!-- page NNN -->` comment marks the start of each page's text. `book-of-mormon-1830.txt` is the same with all markup removed. `book-of-mormon-1830-sparse.txt` is the Book of Mormon text only, limited to the PDF ranges in `PAGE_RANGES` in `tools/make_sparse.py`: the title page (PDF 5, because it was translated from the plates, without the 1830 imprint paragraphs at its foot) and PDF 9–592. (Changed at the owner's request after the first build, which started at PDF 9.) **A compile bug was found and fixed late:** where a page ends in the middle of a paragraph and also carries a signature-mark footer (`---` rule + `Footer:` line), the rule line was mistaken for the paragraph being continued, so the next page's first word was glued onto it as `--word` and the real paragraph was left unjoined (about 60 places in each of the `.md`, `.txt` and sparse files, for example "the Laman-" … "--ites"). `tools/compile_markdown.py` now drops the rule line before joining; the three compiled files were rebuilt (Markdown 273,395 words, plain text 270,890, sparse 269,535) and checked: no `--` fragments, no unjoined hyphen at a paragraph end, and only the two Internet Archive notice lines begin in lowercase. The page files were never affected. `README.md` was edited by hand by the owner after the AI draft; treat the current text as the owner's and edit it in place, not by regenerating it.
